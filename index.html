15
Signals and guardrails

Watch four numbers. Avoid four traps.

Watch

  • Saves and shares per post. They predict reach better than likes.
  • Profile visits to follows. Tells her whether the bio and grid convert.
  • Reel watch time. Does the first two seconds hold?
  • Enquiries a month. Brand emails, press, and shop visits. The number that pays.

Avoid

  • Bought followers or engagement pods. Brands check, and the algorithm punishes it.
  • Every trend. Only trends she can do in her own style.
  • Unlabelled ads. Japan has banned stealth marketing since 1 October 2023. Sweden requires a clear "reklam" label. Mark every paid post.
  • Live location. Post a place after leaving it. Keep home and family off the grid.
