04
The one habit

One real clip a week.

  • FromSomething already in your week. Seeing Miho. Friday's event. A walk, a dinner, a fitting.
  • Length10 to 30 seconds. A few moments, not a story.
  • LookReal light, light edits. You, as you actually look.
  • VoiceNot needed. Music, or the room's own sound, plus one line of text if you like.
  • WhoKeigo or a friend films when possible. The editor cuts it. You approve and post.

Anyone can retouch a photo. In video, people see that you actually look like that.Sophia's point, and the heart of this plan

07
The content day

Three looks. Half a day. A month of posts.

  • WhenOnce a month, only on a good-energy week. Moving the date is always allowed.
  • WhereOne or two places close together. Less travel, more energy for the camera.
  • TimeAbout four hours, with breaks built in. Hair and makeup done once, adjusted per look.
  • PlanSense Tokyo sends the shot list three days before. You only decide the outfits.

08
Your signature — what to learn from Felicia

Be the one people think of first.

Felicia is the queen of makeup transformations, and Halloween is her big night. You don't need her personality, only the idea: one format you are clearly the best at.

  • CandidateThe glam transformation. Everyday Tokyo to full evening look, in one clip.
  • Why youGlamour is what people already remember you for, since Miss Universe Sweden.
  • First testHalloween, Saturday 31 October. A full-glam look, planned and filmed with help.
  • GrowthPractise it in public. Getting visibly better is recognition for a skill, not just for how you look.

A candidate, not a decision. If another format feels more like you, that one wins.

The fifth door

Host it yourself.

Once a quarter, on a good week: a small brunch for the international crowd you like. It recreates your yacht and Paris moments, gives you something real to share, and shows brands you can fill a room. Later, a brand can sponsor the table.

Honestly: most PR events in Tokyo are made for Japanese audiences, and we have not checked which international PR teams invite English-speaking creators. That is a guess to test in October, one email at a time, not a promise.

Modelling, when it comes

Stay ready. Don't chase.

Past work with Shiseido, Nylon Japan, and Vogue Japan still counts. The job now is to be easy to book.

  • Digitals: fresh natural shots every six months, taken on a content day.
  • Comp card: one current PDF with measurements and your best frames.
  • Agency: Keigo checks whether your Tokyo representation is still active.
  • Feed: the weekly clips show casting how you move.
